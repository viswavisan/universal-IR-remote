ZONE 1: TOP 3-PIN 5V IR TRANSMITTER & BOTTOM 3-PIN IR RECEIVER

Physically separated optics (170mm distance): Top 5V blaster for appliances, Bottom receiver for zero-crosstalk learning:

Top Module: 3-Pin 5V High-Power IR LED Blaster
• Pin 1 (5V) ➜ Pico Pin 40 (VBUS 5V) High-Power Rail
• Pin 2 (GND) ➜ Pico Pin 18 (GND) Ground Return
• Pin 3 (IN) ➜ Pico GP14 (Pin 19) 38kHz PWM Carrier (Slice 7A)
⚡ Powered by 5V rail via onboard driver transistor for ~100mA pulses and 10–15m range!
Bottom Module: 3-Pin VS1838B IR Learning Eye
• Pin 1 (OUT) ➜ Pico GP11 (Pin 15) Demodulated Active-LOW Data IN
• Pin 2 (GND) ➜ Pico Pin 13 (GND) Ground Return
• Pin 3 (VCC) ➜ Pico GP10 (Pin 14) 3.3V Power Out (driven HIGH)
Captures and learns raw 38kHz infrared signals from original remotes pointed at bottom window.
ZONE 2: DIRECT 8-PIN 4x4 KEYPAD MATRIX (NO I2C)

8 direct GPIO connections using RP2040 internal pull-ups (zero extra chips):

4 Rows (Outputs - Driven LOW during scan):
• Row 0 (Pin 1) ➜ Pico GP2 (Pin 4)
• Row 1 (Pin 2) ➜ Pico GP3 (Pin 5)
• Row 2 (Pin 3) ➜ Pico GP4 (Pin 6)
• Row 3 (Pin 4) ➜ Pico GP5 (Pin 7)
4 Columns (Inputs with Internal Pull-Up):
• Col 0 (Pin 5) ➜ Pico GP6 (Pin 9)
• Col 1 (Pin 6) ➜ Pico GP7 (Pin 10)
• Col 2 (Pin 7) ➜ Pico GP8 (Pin 11)
• Col 3 (Pin 8) ➜ Pico GP9 (Pin 12)
ZONE 3: 4.5V POWER SUPPLY, BOTTOM USB & IR LEARN PORT
3x AAA Battery Pack & Thumb Slide Switch
• Red Wire (+) ➜ Slide Switch IN (3x AAA = 4.5V DC)
• Switch OUT ➜ Pico VSYS (Pin 39)
• Black Wire (-) ➜ Pico GND (Pin 38)
Zero quiescent battery drain when switch is turned OFF
Bottom Bezel Ports & 3D Casing Specs
• Bottom USB Port: Direct flashing cable access
• Bottom IR Learn Port: VS1838B optical window
