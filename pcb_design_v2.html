• 100% Direct Vertical Rails: All 8 column buses run straight up into Pico GPIOs without a single bend!
• Symmetrical Keypad: Buttons 1–64 (4.5mm, 3×2 holes) centered across 22 columns with equal 2-col margins.
• Clear Internal Pin Labels: Labels are positioned inside the Pico PCB body for high contrast and zero clutter.
• F20 Crossover Jumper: GP4 front-side lead hops over Row 20 bus at F20 with complete electrical isolation.
• Dedicated System Keys: Button 2 = LEARN (Amber), Button 3 = MODE (Emerald).
• Rechargeable Power Architecture: 3.7V 14500 Li-ion cell (AA Size) + TP4056 USB-C charger module directly powering Pico VSYS with DW01A protection.
